Straight answers

The questions every shop owner asks us first.

Do you fake reviews or pay for them?

Never. Bought and faked reviews violate Google's rules, get shops suspended, and fool nobody for long. Our whole service is built on the opposite: a real asking system that turns your genuinely happy customers into real reviews.

Do I have to give you my password?

No. You add us as a manager on your Google Business Profile and Facebook page using your own login. You keep full ownership, and you can remove our access in about a minute, anytime.

What if I get a bad review?

It gets a calm, professional reply — the kind that future customers judge you by, often more than the review itself. And the best defense is volume: a steady stream of real, happy reviews puts one bad day in its proper place.

How much of my time does this take?

Almost none. A short setup call, a photo of finished jobs when you remember to snap one, and five minutes with the monthly report. The asking, replying, posting, and checking are on us.

Is there a contract?

No contract and no setup fee. Your $199 rate is locked for your first six months; after that it moves to the regular rate, and you can cancel anytime before a billing date.

Who is this for — and not for?

It's built for single-location home service businesses: plumbing, HVAC, electrical, roofing, and the like. If you run multiple locations or a franchise, we're probably not your shop — and we'll tell you so on the audit call.
